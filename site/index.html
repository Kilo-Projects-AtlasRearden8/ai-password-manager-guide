Welcome

This beginner-friendly manual walks you through passwords, password managers, two-factor authentication, and securing your accounts with AI help.

Written in plain English, step by step, never condescending. Published by Asdale Dynamics, LLC.

What this guide covers

  1. Choosing a password manager — pick one that fits your needs and get it set up
  2. Two-factor authentication — what it is and how to turn it on
  3. Securing your accounts with AI help — use AI as a helper, not a replacement for your judgment
  4. Printable security checklist — track your progress on a single printable page

Quick start

  1. Pick a password manager and install it
  2. Create one strong master password
  3. Save your existing logins into the manager
  4. Turn on two-factor authentication for your important accounts
  5. Use the printable checklist to track your progress
Why this matters: A strong, unique password for every account — plus a password manager to remember them — is one of the best things you can do for your security.

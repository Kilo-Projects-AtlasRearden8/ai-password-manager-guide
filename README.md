# AI Password Manager Guide

A beginner-friendly manual on passwords, password managers, two-factor authentication, and securing your accounts with AI help. Plain English, step by step, never condescending.

Published by [Asdale Dynamics, LLC](https://dylanasdale.github.io) — beginner-friendly AI education, books, guides, and practical tools.

## About this guide

This guide helps anyone protect their online accounts without needing a computer science degree. It walks you through:

1. **Choosing a password manager** that fits your needs
2. **Two-factor authentication** — what it is and how to turn it on
3. **Securing your accounts with AI help** — using AI as a helper, not a replacement for your judgment
4. **A printable security checklist** to track your progress

Written by Dylan Asdale, a registered nurse turned AI author and founder of Asdale Dynamics, LLC (Indiana, formed October 2026). Asdale Dynamics focuses on beginner-friendly AI education, practical tools, and accessible life-admin helpers. This guide is part of that mission: clear, warm, polished, tested, local-first.

## How to use

**Option 1 — Read the markdown:** Open any `.md` file in the `docs/` folder.

**Option 2 — Read the website:** Open `site/index.html` in your web browser. All pages are static HTML — no build step, no server, no internet connection required after download.

**To print the checklist:** Open `site/security-checklist.html` and click "Print this checklist," or use your browser's print command. Print styles hide navigation and show only the checklist content.

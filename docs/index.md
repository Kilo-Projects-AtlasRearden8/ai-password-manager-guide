Anyone who wants to protect their online accounts without needing a computer science degree. If you've ever reused the same password on multiple sites, forgotten a password, or worried about getting hacked, this guide is for you.

---

## Why passwords matter

Your password is the key to your digital life — email, banking, shopping, social media, and more. If someone guesses or steals your password, they can:

- Read your email
- Reset passwords on other accounts
- Make purchases with your saved cards
- Lock you out of your own accounts

A strong, unique password for every account — plus a password manager to remember them — is one of the best things you can do for your security.

## Quick start

1. Pick a password manager (see the next page)
2. Install it on your phone and computer
3. Create one strong master password
4. Save your existing logins into the manager
5. Turn on two-factor authentication wherever you can
6. Use the printable checklist to track your progress
